# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Updated the original ElvUI Chat Textures addon for Retail 12.1.0.
- Refreshed the crest artwork and expanded the collection to 40 class, faction, and racial backgrounds, including Demon Hunter, Evoker, and Earthen.
- Softened each crest to 40% opacity on a black 256 × 128 background so your chat gets the spotlight.
- Added `/ect` to open a movable, scrollable texture catalog with Blizzard's default styling and your ElvUI general font.
- Grouped and alphabetized the paths, with click-to-select fields and clear directions for copying them into both chat panels.
- Preserved existing texture filenames and chat settings.
